Master's thesis

In progress · TUM

Reinforcement Learning for Sampling-Based Trajectory Planning

Autonomous Vehicle Systems group (Prof. Johannes Betz), advised by Mattia Piccinini

PPO policyadapts the planner online, during the race
→
Cost weights, velocity samplinglog-space simplex; centre of the longitudinal velocity samples
→
Frenet sampling plannerfeasibility checked before any learned cost is evaluated
→
Trajectoryfeasible by construction
  • A PPO policy adapts online the cost weights of a Frenet-frame sampling-based planner for autonomous racing, through a log-space simplex parametrisation, and shifts the centre of the planner's longitudinal velocity sampling distribution.
  • The policy reshapes how the planner searches instead of emitting trajectories: kinematic and dynamic feasibility is enforced by construction before any learned cost is evaluated, making the safety argument structural rather than statistical.
  • Integrated into the group's shared benchmarking framework and evaluated against four other internal planners on identical scenarios.

Quadrotor RL on a Massively Parallel JAX Simulator

A quadrotor simulator in JAX that flies tens of thousands of vehicles in parallel on one GPU (192 M steps/s), with PPO written from scratch on top of it and compared with tuned classical controllers. On random gate courses the learned policy needs about 40 % less time than a tuned pursuit controller and finishes 98 % of them. In 1-vs-1 pursuit-evasion trained by self-play, the learned pursuer catches a classical evader in 38 % less time than a classical chaser, though it still crashes more often.
