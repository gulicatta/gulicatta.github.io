Reinforcement Learning for Sampling-Based Trajectory Planning

TUM Autonomous Vehicle Systems (Prof. Johannes Betz), advised by Mattia Piccinini

PPO policyadapts the planner online, during the race
→
Cost weights, velocity samplinglog-space simplex; centre of the longitudinal velocity samples
→
Frenet sampling plannerfeasibility checked before any learned cost is evaluated
→
Trajectoryfeasible by construction
  • A PPO policy adapts online the cost weights of a Frenet-frame sampling-based planner for autonomous racing, through a log-space simplex parametrisation, and shifts the centre of the planner's longitudinal velocity sampling distribution.
  • The policy reshapes how the planner searches instead of emitting trajectories: kinematic and dynamic feasibility is enforced by construction before any learned cost is evaluated, making the safety argument structural rather than statistical.
  • Integrated into the group's shared benchmarking framework and evaluated against four other internal planners on identical scenarios.

Quadrotor RL on a Massively Parallel JAX Simulator

A quadrotor simulator in JAX that flies tens of thousands of vehicles in parallel on one GPU (192 M steps/s), and PPO written from scratch on top of it, compared with tuned classical controllers. On random gate courses the learned policy is about 40 % faster than a pursuit controller while finishing 98 % of them; now training pursuer and evader drones by self-play.

Adversarial Scenario Generation for UAV Autonomy

With Roberto Capone, for the UAV Testing Competition (ICST / SBFT 2026): a search-based test generator that evolves obstacle configurations to drive PX4-Avoidance into safety-distance violations, using a (1+1)-ES with 1/5-rule step-size adaptation, a targeted "trap" mutation and diversity-driven restarts. Failures are deduplicated by DTW distance between trajectories and executed in a crash-recoverable Docker pipeline on the Aerialist framework.

Autonomous Intersection Management Simulation

A Python simulator for decentralised intersection management: kinematic bicycle model with pure-pursuit tracking, predictive collision avoidance on other vehicles' upcoming waypoints, right-of-way logic and a deadlock breaker. Ten stress scenarios and a headless regression suite checking that no vehicles overlap and all clear the intersection.

IoT-Based Patient Monitoring System

An end-to-end monitoring system on TI CC1350: a SensorTag streams sensor packets every 500 ms over sub-GHz RF to a LaunchPad gateway, forwarded over UART to the backend. Embedded C on TI-RTOS for fall detection, temperature and humidity monitoring and medication reminders; a Flask REST backend and a React dashboard, packaged with Docker Compose.
