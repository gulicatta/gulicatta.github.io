Master's thesis

Reinforcement Learning for Sampling-Based Trajectory Planning

TUM Autonomous Vehicle Systems (Prof. Johannes Betz), advised by Mattia Piccinini · in progress

  • A PPO policy adapts online the cost weights of a Frenet-frame sampling-based planner for autonomous racing, through a log-space simplex parametrisation, and shifts the centre of the planner's longitudinal velocity sampling.
  • The policy reshapes how the planner searches instead of emitting trajectories: kinematic and dynamic feasibility is enforced by construction before any learned cost is evaluated, so the safety argument is structural, not statistical.
  • Integrated into the group's shared benchmarking framework and evaluated against four other internal planners on identical scenarios.

Quadrotor RL on a Massively Parallel JAX Simulator

2026 · ongoing

A quadrotor simulator in JAX that flies tens of thousands of vehicles in parallel on one GPU (192 M steps/s), and PPO written from scratch on top of it. Compared with tuned classical controllers: a PID stays more precise at hovering, but on random gate courses the learned policy is about 40 % faster than a pursuit controller while finishing 98 % of them. Now training pursuer and evader drones against each other by self-play.

Adversarial Scenario Generation for UAV Autonomy

Jul 2026 · Politecnico di Milano, with Roberto Capone · UAV Testing Competition (ICST / SBFT 2026)

A search-based test generator that evolves obstacle configurations to drive PX4-Avoidance into safety-distance violations: a (1+1)-ES with 1/5-rule step-size adaptation, a targeted "trap" mutation and diversity-driven restarts. Failures are kept in a bounded archive deduplicated by DTW distance between trajectories, re-run near the threshold to discount simulator noise, and executed in a crash-recoverable Docker pipeline on the Aerialist framework.

Autonomous Intersection Management Simulation

Apr 2026

A Python simulator for decentralised intersection management: kinematic bicycle model with pure-pursuit tracking, predictive collision avoidance on other vehicles' upcoming waypoints, right-of-way logic and a deadlock breaker. Ten stress scenarios (3- and 4-vehicle gridlocks, multiple simultaneous left turns, seeded random traffic) and a headless regression suite checking that no vehicles overlap and all clear the intersection.

Machine-learning course project
